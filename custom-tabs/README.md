# Custom Sidebar Tabs

Add your own sidebar tabs and fill them with the panes you want side by side: Source Control next to Tasks, Search next to Ports, or whatever fits how you work. Each tab has a name and an icon.

## Managing tabs

You can do everything from any of three places:

- **Tab strip right-click.** Right-click a sidebar tab, or the empty part of the tab strip, and choose **New Tab…**. On one of your tabs the same menu also has **Rename…**, **Change Icon…** and **Delete Tab…**. A tab created here joins the sidebar you right-clicked.
- **Command palette.** **Sidebar: New Tab…**, **Sidebar: Rename Tab…**, **Sidebar: Change Tab Icon…** and **Sidebar: Delete Tab…**.
- **Settings.** This extension's section lists your tabs with the same actions, plus **Add Tab**.

A new tab asks for a name, then an icon. The icon picker searches icon names and their descriptions, so typing "person" finds the account icon.

## Filling a tab

Drag a pane's header onto the tab's icon, or right-click the pane header and choose **Move to** your tab. Drag the tab itself to reorder it or to move it to the other sidebar. Your tabs, their icons, where they sit and what's in them follow you to your other devices.

## Deleting vs. disabling

- **Deleting** a tab sends every pane in it back to where it was before you moved it.
- **Disabling or uninstalling** the extension hides your tabs, and their panes show in their usual places meanwhile. Turn the extension back on and the tabs come back with the same panes in them.

## Requirements

A Perch with sidebar tab support. On an older Perch the extension adds nothing and its Settings section says it needs an update.
